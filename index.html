Stream moments, cut into Shorts.

Clipping is a private, single-user tool. It turns highlights from a live gaming stream into vertical clips and uploads the ones its owner approves to the owner's own YouTube channel.

WAIT FOR IT

How it works

  1. It records a live stream and watches how fast the chat moves.
  2. When the chat spikes, it cuts a 30-second vertical clip and adds captions.
  3. The owner reviews every clip and approves or skips it by hand.
  4. Only an approved clip is uploaded to the owner's YouTube channel, with the YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

Only its owner. Clipping runs on the owner's own computer, has no public sign-up and no other users, and never accesses anyone else's account.
